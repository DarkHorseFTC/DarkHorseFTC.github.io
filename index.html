How the herd works

Grow

Discovery and innovation, every meeting. We explore new skills and ideas together, and use creativity and persistence to solve problems. Every prototype — including the ones that don't work yet — teaches us something worth keeping.

Compete with heart

Gracious Professionalism® and Coopertition®. We compete hard and cheer harder — sharing parts, knowledge, and encouragement with every team we meet. Helping each other is the best tradition in FIRST.

Belong

Inclusion, teamwork, and fun. Every teammate matters and every voice shapes the robot. We respect each other, embrace our differences, and celebrate what we build — because we're stronger together, and this is supposed to be joyful.
