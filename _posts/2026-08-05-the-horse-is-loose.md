title: "The horse is loose"
---

DarkHorse Robotics exists. A rookie FIRST Tech Challenge team of 6th–8th graders
at Meridian Creek Middle School is officially saddling up for the 2026–27
**BIOBUZZ** season.

What happens next:

- **September 12** — kickoff. The game reveals, and we find out what we're building.
- **September 19** — FirstFare at Portland State, where we learn from Oregon's veteran teams.
- **October 24** — League Meet 0, in our own gym.

This log will track the build — the wins, the breakages, and what we learned
from each. Mostly the breakages, at first. That's the plan.
